# Marvel Product Catalogue and Product Sheets

> Marvel CPG is a fictional company created for the University at Buffalo CDA 650 capstone. This document was written by Team 1 for the project's RAG knowledge base. Figures marked *data* come from the project dataset; everything else is fictional business content.

## 1 About this catalogue

Marvel sells 23 household cleaning SKUs in three categories: Surface Cleaners (8 SKUs), Dishwashing (7) and Laundry (8). They are sold under six brands: Marvel Clean, Marvel Pro, Marvel Bright, Marvel Fresh, Marvel Wash and Marvel Soft. 15 of the 23 SKUs are on the Must-Stock List (MSL), which means every account is expected to keep them on the shelf (see MCPG-06).

Use this catalogue to answer product questions from store owners and buyers: what a product is for, how to use it, what to be careful about, and how it performs commercially. Each product sheet has a fixed layout so the same question always finds the same answer:

- **What it is** and **How to use**: product information. This is fictional content written for the project.
- **Cautions**: safety and label points. Always defer to the printed product label (see PUB-03 for general safe-use guidance).
- **Commercial facts**: prices from the product master and performance figures from the project dataset (marked *data*). Section 5 explains how each figure is calculated.

Category value shares in the data period *(data)*: Laundry 39.6%, Surface Cleaners 32.2%, Dishwashing 28.2% of consumer sales value. MSL SKUs make up 78% of value, and the three biggest SKUs by value are Laundry Pods 42ct, Dishwasher Pods 45ct and Laundry Detergent HE 100oz.

### 5.2 How the commercial facts are defined

- **Trade list price** is the price Marvel charges the account (retailer, chain or distributor) before any trade discount. It is the basis for all discount and margin calculations (MCPG-05).
- **Reference consumer shelf price** is the planning reference for what shoppers pay. It is 1.30 to 1.40 times the list price for every SKU. Retailers set their own shelf prices, so treat it as a reference, not a promise.
- **Marvel gross margin at list price** = (list price − unit cost) ÷ list price. Every SKU sits between 51% and 54% before discounts.
- **Consumer sales value** = units sold × the price the shopper actually paid, over reported store-days from 1 January 2025 to 31 May 2026.
- **Average units per in-stock store-day** counts only days when the store reported and the SKU did not end the day out of stock.
- **Out-of-stock rate** = reported store-SKU-days that ended with zero inventory ÷ all reported store-SKU-days. Days a store did not report are Unknown and are not counted (MCPG-08).
- **Accounts that carry it** = accounts with at least one reported sales row for the SKU. Non-MSL SKUs are carried by 124 to 141 of the 221 accounts.
